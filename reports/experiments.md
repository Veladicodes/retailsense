# Experiment report

Generated by `python -m retailsense.pipeline.run_experiments`. Every number below is measured, not hand-entered.

## Protocol
- Task: one-week-ahead demand per SKU, 100 SKUs, 67 features, rolling origin.
- Split: train -> validation (13 weeks) -> untouched holdout (13 weeks, 2011-09-05 to 2011-11-28).
- Model selection: lowest validation SKU-level WAPE (models and baselines alike); holdout scored once after refit on train+val.
- MAPE is computed over rows with actual demand > 0; WAPE (sum abs error / sum actual) is shown too.
- Configurations compared: 23 fitted models + 4 naive baselines.
